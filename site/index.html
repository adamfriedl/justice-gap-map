How to read this

  • One county, one kind of court. Harris County's 16 justice courts hear the smaller claims. Larger debt suits go to county and district courts and aren't here.
  • Courts move at different speeds. In some courts most 2025 cases are closed; in others most are still open. Outcome shares for a plaintiff partly reflect which courts it files in. The table above shows each court.
  • Kinds of plaintiff are this project's grouping, made from company names. It isn't from a registry and hasn't been checked against one. 2.9% of cases have a plaintiff that isn't classified.
  • "Dropped by the plaintiff" is the courts' category "non-suited or dismissed by plaintiff". The records don't say why a case was dropped.
  • A case is counted once, at its latest status in the county's extract downloaded Nov 11, 2026. The county's count for 2025 here is 94,999; the state's Office of Court Administration reports 91,823 debt claims filed in Harris County justice courts in 2025, about 3% fewer.

Source: Harris County Justice Courts public data extract. Tables and code: docs/docket in the repository.
